OWMETA HUD recognizes scoreboards on your PC with the components below. Each keeps its own license.

|---|---|---|
| [ONNX Runtime](https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime) (Microsoft.ML.OnnxRuntime) | Runs the portrait encoder | MIT |
| [Tesseract OCR](https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tesseract) and the [.NET wrapper](https://github.com/charlesw/tesseract) | Reads the map and hero names | Apache-2.0 |
| [Leptonica](http://www.leptonica.org/) | Image library used by Tesseract | BSD 2-Clause |
